Getting started

  1. Open Volna and allow the microphone.
  2. Download a speech model on the Models page (Volna suggests the best one for your languages).
  3. On Home, click the microphone and speak: the text appears in the Try It field.
  4. In any app, press your dictation key or shortcut, speak, and press it again. In the Mac App Store version the shortcut is Option-Space; the version from our website can also use a single key, such as the right Command key.

Common questions

Where does my text go? In the Mac App Store version, Volna copies it and shows "Copied: press ⌘V"; paste it with Command-V. The version from our website can also type it straight into the field.

Does Volna send my voice anywhere? No. Recognition runs on your Mac; see the privacy policy.

Which languages does it understand? About 100 languages. Choose your main language and an optional second one on Home; each phrase is recognized in whichever of the two you speak.

The text has mistakes. Add names and terms in the Dictionary, or try a larger model on the Models page.
