Volna Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

In short: Volna turns your speech into text entirely on your Mac. Your voice and your text never leave your Mac. Volna has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we collect no personal data.

This policy explains how Volna, the voice-to-text app for Mac made by Stillbyte LLC ("we"), handles information. It covers Volna from the Mac App Store and Volna downloaded from our website.

Your voice and your text stay on your Mac

Volna records audio from your microphone only while you dictate, and recognizes the speech with speech models that run on your Mac. The audio and the resulting text are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Volna keeps on your Mac

  • History. Off by default. If you turn on Save History, your dictations are kept on your Mac so you can find and reuse them. You can turn it off, delete single dictations, or delete all of them with Clear All.
  • Audio. Off by default. If you turn on Save Audio, the sound of each dictation is kept on your Mac with its text, and can be deleted automatically after a period you choose.
  • Your settings, dictionary and training samples are stored on your Mac.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. If you remove Volna, you can delete this data with the app or by removing its folder in your Library.

When Volna uses the internet

  • Downloading speech models. Volna downloads the speech recognition models you choose from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and standard request information. Volna sends no audio, text or personal data with these requests.
  • Checking for updates (the version from our website only). Volna checks for new versions at updates.ivolna.com, and new versions are downloaded from our GitHub Pages site. These requests carry no personal data. The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store instead.

Volna makes no other network requests.

Permissions

  • Microphone is required to hear what you dictate.
  • Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility before macOS 27) and Input Monitoring are optional and used only in the version from our website: to type the text into the field where your cursor is and to let a single key start dictation. Volna reacts only to its own dictation keys and never stores what you type. The Mac App Store version does not use them.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data, and Volna contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you.

Children

Volna does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date above.
